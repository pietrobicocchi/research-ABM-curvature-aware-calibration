## Question

Validate `∇²L = G + R` on controlled nonlinear problems, and find the
neighborhood where the GGN predicts local loss geometry.

## Method

Two cases, squared-residual loss, float64: **A** Rosenbrock exact-fit valley
(`R=0` at optimum); **B** irreducible 3-in-2 residual (analytic `R=2λr₃I`, nonzero
at the minimizer). Prespecified signed α-grid, `τ=0.10` validity radius.

## Result

- AD `H`, `G`, `R` vs analytic: `≤ 3.2e-15` — decomposition numerically exact.
- Case A: `H=G` at the exact fit; validity radii `ρ∈{0.05…0.3}`, shrinking with curvature.
- Case B: at the nonzero-residual minimizer `‖R‖/‖H‖` up to 0.16; validity radius
  **collapses to 0** under a constant relative bias `≈ vᵀRv/vᵀHv` (irreducible as α→0).
- Two failure modes distinguished: (1) residual-curvature bias (nonzero as α→0,
  measured by `e_curv`); (2) higher-order error (grows with radius).
