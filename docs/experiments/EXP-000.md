## Question

What did the historical Brock–Hommes OPG measure? Compare, at matched points in
prior-scaled `z` with common random numbers, the true GGN `G = J_ηᵀ J_η`, the
exact Hessian `H`, the per-seed scalar-gradient second moment `F_OPG`, and the
centered gradient covariance `C_g`.

## Method

BH in the **correct rich regime** (R=1.01, σ=0.04): **P1** β=50 (complex, ac₁=0.82),
**P2** β=80 (chaotic, ac₁=0.90, traj-std ≈ 12× noise). Finite-feature (RFF)
mean-embedding for `G`/`H`; historical U-statistic MMD² per-seed gradients for
`F_OPG`/`C_g`. **Gradient-horizon truncation (H=20) applied uniformly to all four
matrices** — required because full-horizon pathwise gradients explode in the
chaotic regime. Residual sweep along the stiff direction, `B=15`, float64.

> Supersedes the first EXP-000 run, which used β=2–5 (trivial near-fundamental
> regime → rank-1 artifact). BH chaos needs large β; see
> `papers/reference_brock_hommes_1998.md`.

## Result

- **G is genuinely multi-directional** (effective rank ~4), not rank-1.
- **The historical `F_OPG` is NOT the GGN.** `F_OPG = C_g + ḡḡᵀ`: the covariance
  part dominates the subspace, plus a residual-driven rank-1 term.
  - **P2 (chaotic):** `F_OPG ≈ C_g` (rel-fro 0.008, top-2 angle **0.07°**) and is
    **nearly orthogonal to the true GGN** (top-2 angle **86°**). The historical
    analysis measured stochastic-gradient variability, not curvature — **Outcome B**.
  - **P1 (complex):** `F_OPG`'s top-2 subspace sits ~11° from *both* `G` and `C_g`;
    at zero residual it aligns with `C_g` (1°), not `G` (79°). A residual-dependent
    blend, never the GGN.
- **Full-horizon GGN is numerically inaccessible** in the chaotic regime:
  feature-Jacobian norm explodes by **~1.7×10⁴** (β=80) vs the truncated horizon.
  Differentiation-horizon truncation is required and defines a distinct (finite)
  geometry (RQ4).
- `H` vs `G`: `R/‖H‖ ≈ 1` at nonzero residual — GGN ≠ Hessian away from the fit
  (consistent with EXP-002); `H=G` at zero residual.

## Claim impact

- **C08 → not supported** — the old OPG eigenspaces do **not** align with the true
  GGN on Brock–Hommes; `F_OPG` tracks the gradient covariance `C_g` and is nearly
  orthogonal to `G` in the chaotic regime.
- Reinforces **C07** (rejected): the scalar OPG is not a GGN estimator.
