# EXP-006 — SIR policy-functional analysis

**Status:** done
**Authoritative code commit:** `91fe277` (run `outputs/EXP-006/*_91fe277/`; deterministic)
**Supports:** C12

## Question

Does data non-identifiability leave a **policy** output undetermined? I.e. can a
policy functional move materially while the fit stays good — and is that movement
along a **sloppy** (low prior-relative-eigenvalue) GGN direction?

## Method

Smooth SIR from EXP-005 (same transform, θ*, loss, prior-scaled z). Policy
functionals: peak incidence, total cases, and the **intervention value** (cases
averted by the lockdown = total(f_lock=1) − total(actual)). For each functional
trace the profiled fit cost vs the policy value,

    P(q) = min_{z : Q(z)=q} L(z),

by a Lagrange-multiplier sweep (`min_z L(z) − μ Q(z)`, L-BFGS). Fit budget = 2 nats
(residual well within noise). Report the achievable policy range within budget and
which GGN eigen-direction the movement aligns with. float64, deterministic.

## Result

Prior-relative spectrum `[2.5e4, 135, 2.78, 5.3e-3, 8.9e-6]`, `d_data = 3`.

| policy functional | in-fit range (within 2 nats) | along GGN dir |
|---|---|---|
| total cases (observed) | **±0.3%** — tightly identified | — |
| peak incidence (observed) | **±0.2%** — tightly identified | — |
| **intervention value (counterfactual)** | **0 → ~2180 cases** (mode 90; +2300%) at fit cost 0.70 | **sloppiest**, λ=8.9e-6 (f_lock) |

The directly-observed quantities are pinned down by the data. The **counterfactual
intervention value is wildly unidentified**: the same excellent epidemic-curve fit
is consistent with the lockdown averting **~0 cases or ~2180 cases** — a
policy-decisive difference — and this freedom lies along the **sloppiest GGN
direction** (loading on `f_lock`, the lockdown strength, which the incidence data
barely constrains because the lockdown falls after the peak at θ*).

## Claim impact

- **C12 → supported conditionally:** on smooth SIR, a locally weak (sloppy,
  prior-dominated) GGN direction materially changes a policy quantity (the
  intervention value spans ~0→2180 cases within a good fit), while observed
  quantities are tightly identified. The GGN weak direction correctly flags the
  policy answer the data cannot determine.
- Scope: smooth deterministic SIR, this observation design and θ*. A different
  observation design (e.g. observing during/after the lockdown) would likely
  constrain f_lock — the subject of EXP-007.
