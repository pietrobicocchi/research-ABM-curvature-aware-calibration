## Question

On the *stochastic, discrete* network-SIR (the model family whose AD we validated
against Quera-Bofarull 2025), two questions of the local GGN geometry:

- **C14** — is the important (stiff) eigenspace **robust to the chosen surrogate
  gradient**? Compare the GGN under Gumbel-Sigmoid vs straight-through — the two
  discrete-randomness estimators our repo shares with Quera-Bofarull 2025
  (§3.4.2 / §3.4.1); SPA/StochasticAD (§3.4.3) deferred per DEC-011.
- **C15** — does **truncated differentiation** (`grad_horizon`) destroy that
  geometry?

## Method

Model: `models/network_sir.py`, P=5, at θ\* = (β 0.03, γ 0.06, I0_frac 0.03,
t_lock_norm 0.20, f_lock 0.30) on a fixed ER graph (N=150, ⟨k⟩=6, `graph_seed=17`),
T=40, dt=1. This is a **partial** epidemic (attack rate ≈29%, peak incidence ≈2.3,
lockdown bends the curve ~48%) so all five parameters shape the incidence curve and
the GGN is full-rank (5 nonzero eigenvalues).

Representation: MMD mean embedding `m(z) = E_ξ[φ_RFF(incidence(z,ξ))]`, frozen RFF
(D=128, median-heuristic bandwidth 4.73, DEC-006), prior-whitened coords z∼N(0,I)
(DEC-003). GGN `G = J_mᵀ J_m` via the plug-in mean-Jacobian estimator (EXP-003 Ĝ_V),
M=128 seeds with common random numbers, **float64**. Cross-surrogate agreement =
principal angles of leading-k eigenspaces; horizon sweep on the smooth (Gumbel)
surrogate, grad_horizon ∈ {40,20,10,5}, angle vs the full-horizon reference.

FD validation (Quera-Bofarull 2025 §5.3.1 protocol): each surrogate's mean-embedding
Jacobian vs a central finite-difference reference on the **seed-averaged** embedding
with common random numbers (step 1e-2).

## Result

| surrogate | GGN eigenvalues (desc) | FD median rel-Jac error |
|---|---|---|
| Gumbel-Sigmoid | 3.9e-1, 5.5e-2, 1.2e-2, 5.8e-4, 4.0e-4 | **3.1e-5** (validated) |
| straight-through | 1.7e-1, 1.2e-2, 3.5e-3, 2.3e-4, 1.3e-4 | 1.20 (see note) |

- **C14 — leading eigenspace robust across surrogates.** Principal angle between the
  Gumbel and straight-through eigenspaces: **8.6° (k=1), 8.9° (k=2), 5.9° (k=3)**.
  The *directions* the diagnostic calls stiff/sloppy are nearly surrogate-invariant.
  The *scale* is not: straight-through eigenvalues are 0.22–0.43× the Gumbel ones
  (smaller GGN), so absolute curvature magnitude is surrogate-dependent.
- **Gumbel AD is finite-difference-validated** (median rel-Jac error 3.1e-5): the
  discrete-SIR AD GGN construction is correct on the smooth surrogate. The
  straight-through Jacobian does **not** match FD (1.20) — expected: its per-path
  forward is piecewise-constant in z, so the FD reference on the seed-averaged
  embedding is high-variance at M=128, and ST is a known-biased estimator. So the
  C14 agreement means "a biased surrogate reproduces the FD-validated leading
  directions," not that ST is itself FD-verifiable here.
- **C15 — horizon truncation destroys the geometry.** Leading-2 eigenspace angle vs
  the full-horizon GGN, with top-eigenvalue ratio: gh=40 **0°** (1.00); gh=20
  **80.3°** (0.15); gh=10 **81.6°** (0.035); gh=5 **81.7°** (0.014). Truncating
  differentiation to trailing steps — past the epidemic's informative growth phase —
  both rotates the stiff subspace ~80° and collapses its eigenvalue to 1–15% of full.
  The simultaneous angle-blowup **and** eigenvalue-collapse is genuine destruction of
  the local information geometry, not a mere rotation. Direct substantiation of RQ4
  (DEC-011): pathwise-differentiated geometry is only meaningful over a horizon that
  covers the informative dynamics.

## Claim impact

- **C14 → supported conditionally:** on stochastic discrete network-SIR, the leading
  GGN eigenspace is robust to the surrogate gradient (Gumbel↔straight-through ≤8.9°
  for k≤3), with the Gumbel construction FD-validated (3.1e-5); the eigenvalue
  *scale* is surrogate-dependent (0.22–0.43×). Scope: this θ\*, D=128 RFF/median
  bandwidth, M=128, Gumbel-vs-ST only (SPA deferred, DEC-011); ST not FD-verifiable
  at this M.
- **C15 → supported conditionally:** truncated differentiation destroys the local
  information geometry — halving the horizon rotates the leading eigenspace ~80° and
  collapses its curvature to ~15% of full; shorter horizons to ~1%. Scope: this θ\*
  and transient epidemic; Gumbel surrogate.
- Both remain **conditional** — single operating point, one graph, RFF/MMD
  representation, SPA estimator not yet included.
